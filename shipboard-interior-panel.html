Project overview · For program offices and technical evaluators

Shipboard Interior Panel

Kinetic Perimeter Systems is developing a panel concept for shipboard interior partitions. KPS seeks direct engagement with DoD program offices and technical evaluators to define the requirements for a physical prototype and a side-by-side comparison with a conventional marine joiner panel.

Concept developed. Physical prototype and testing pending.

Current work and proposed next step

The current stage is concept development. A physical prototype and testing are pending. KPS is seeking a program-office conversation to define the requirements, comparison conditions, and appropriate evaluation approach before prototype work proceeds.

The proposed evaluation would compare a prototype panel with a conventional marine joiner panel. No performance results, qualification, award, Navy endorsement, production capacity, or delivery date are claimed.

Evaluation questions

These are questions for the program office and technical evaluators to scope—not claims of achieved performance.

Fire behaviorWhich fire-performance requirements, test methods, and comparison conditions apply to the intended partition use?
SmokeWhich smoke-related measures and test reports would the evaluation require?
Mass per unit areaHow should panel mass per unit area be measured and compared against the selected conventional marine joiner panel?
Handling and installationWhich practical handling, fit-up, installation steps, and representative conditions should evaluators assess side by side?
