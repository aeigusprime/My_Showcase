PROJECT OVERVIEW

Kinetic Perimeter Systems is developing a panel concept for shipboard interior partitions. KPS seeks direct engagement with DoD program offices and technical evaluators to define the requirements for a physical prototype and a side-by-side comparison with a conventional marine joiner panel.

PROPOSED EVALUATION QUESTIONS

  • Fire behavior: Which applicable requirements, test methods, and comparison conditions should be used?
  • Smoke: Which smoke-related measures and test reports should the evaluation address?
  • Mass per unit area: How should the prototype be measured and compared with the selected conventional panel?
  • Handling and installation: Which practical handling, fit-up, installation steps, and representative conditions should be assessed?

ENGAGEMENT

KPS invites program offices and technical evaluators to discuss relevant performance requirements, preferred test reports or sample quantities, and the prototype evaluation or funding pathway their office uses. KPS is the planned manufacturer, subject to requirements and evaluation.
